Event 1

Event Report: Saturday Meet up

Purpose of the Event

  • Share effective AWS learning and hands-on experience for beginners.
  • Introduce tools that help learners practice AWS without incurring high costs.
  • Inspire participants to join technology competitions and Hackathons.
  • Share real-world experiences in developing products from idea to implementation.
  • Help participants build confidence in learning and personal development.

Key Highlights

Learning AWS Efficiently While Saving Costs

Challenges Faced by AWS Beginners

Speaker Huynh Thai Linh discussed common concerns among AWS beginners:

  • Worrying about exceeding AWS budgets.
  • Forgetting to clean up resources after practice sessions, leading to unexpected charges.
  • Fear of extra costs when experimenting with AWS services.
  • Lack of a safe environment for hands-on learning.

Proposed Solutions

AWS Cloud Quest
  • A free hands-on learning platform provided by AWS.
  • Allows learners to complete practical AWS labs.
  • Uses gamification to make learning more engaging.
  • Includes leaderboards that encourage learning through friendly competition.
Floci
  • A local AWS service emulator.
  • Supports testing and practicing AWS services without deploying to real AWS environments.
  • Helps reduce costs during learning and development.

Comparison Between Floci and LocalStack

Advantages of Floci mentioned during the session:

  • Faster performance.
  • Lower memory and storage consumption.
  • Good compatibility with AWS services.
  • More favorable licensing and pricing model.

However, Floci also has several limitations:

  • Supports only a limited number of AWS services.
  • Some services return fake or mock results.
  • Not completely identical to the real AWS environment.
  • Cannot fully replace actual AWS services.

SynthHunter Project

The speakers introduced SynthHunter, a project developed during a Hackathon that achieved excellent results in the competition.

This session helped me better understand the process of developing a real-world technology product and highlighted the importance of teamwork, time management, and problem-solving skills in competitive environments.

Why We Always Need Confidence

Speaker

  • Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu

Main Discussion

This session focused on the importance of confidence in learning and personal growth.

The Cost of Self-Doubt

The speaker explained several negative impacts of lacking confidence:

  • Missing valuable opportunities.
  • Avoiding new challenges.
  • Reduced learning and work performance.
  • Giving up easily when facing difficulties.

The Science Behind the Struggle

Some common reasons people lose confidence include:

  • Fear of failure.
  • Fear of being judged.
  • Focusing too much on outcomes instead of progress.

The 5P Rule

The speaker introduced the 5P Rule as a practical approach to overcoming fear and building confidence in everyday life.

The Power of Confidence

  • Enhances learning ability.
  • Improves work performance.
  • Provides motivation to pursue long-term goals.
  • Encourages people to seize opportunities proactively.

Hacking Everyday Confidence

Several practical strategies were shared:

  • Start with small goals.
  • Celebrate daily achievements.
  • Focus on progress rather than perfection.
  • Maintain a positive mindset and continuous learning attitude.

What I Learned

Technical Knowledge

  • Learned about AWS learning tools such as AWS Cloud Quest and Floci.
  • Understood how to minimize unexpected AWS costs during practice.
  • Recognized the value of AWS emulation environments during development.
  • Gained a better understanding of product development in a Hackathon environment.
  • Learned practical teamwork and time management experiences from real projects.
  • Developed a broader perspective on turning ideas into completed products within a short timeframe.

  • Realized the importance of participating in Hackathons to improve practical skills.
  • Learned how teamwork contributes to successful project development.
  • Better understood the role of confidence in learning and career growth.
  • Recognized that failure is a natural part of personal development.
  • Learned to view challenges as opportunities for growth and improvement.

  • Use AWS Cloud Quest as a safe and cost-effective way to practice AWS.
  • Utilize AWS emulation tools for testing before deploying to real environments.
  • Actively participate in Hackathons to gain experience and expand my knowledge.
  • Apply problem-solving approaches from real-world projects to academic and personal projects.
  • Build greater confidence in learning and developing professional skills.

Event Experience

Attending this workshop was both inspiring and educational. What impressed me most was that the speakers not only shared technical knowledge but also provided valuable real-world experiences from studying, building projects, and participating in technology competitions.

The AWS session helped me realize that beginners can learn and practice AWS effectively by using the right tools and managing resources properly. Learning about AWS Cloud Quest and Floci gave me practical options for improving my AWS skills without worrying too much about costs.

The Hackathon session and the story behind the SynthHunter project gave me a clearer understanding of how a real product can be developed within a limited timeframe. I learned that teamwork, initiative, and problem-solving skills are essential for project success.

The confidence-sharing session also left a strong impression on me. It reminded me that self-doubt can often become the biggest obstacle to personal growth. Being willing to try, learn from mistakes, and continuously improve is crucial for long-term success.

Overall, the workshop not only provided useful knowledge about AWS and real-world project development but also motivated me to continue learning, improving my skills, and building confidence for my future career journey.
